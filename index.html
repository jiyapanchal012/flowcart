What to watch

The call stack executes normal JavaScript immediately. setTimeout schedules callbacks; they run after the current stack is clear and their timer is ready. Each next stage is started inside the previous stage's callback.

All timings are simulated in your browser. Timing may vary depending on the device and browser.
